What to expect

From first conversation to ongoing improvement

The exact scope depends on your project. We agree on responsibilities, timing, and deliverables before work starts.

01
Listen & learn

Discovery

We begin with your business, your customers, your challenges, and your goals. This gives us the context to recommend relevant work rather than assumptions.

  • Discuss your priorities and current marketing
  • Understand your audience and available resources
  • Clarify questions, constraints, and expectations
02
Agree on the work

Direction & planning

We define a useful direction, recommend a realistic scope, and agree how decisions and feedback will be handled.

  • Set objectives and scope
  • Confirm milestones, deliverables, and responsibilities
  • Choose practical ways to review progress
03
Make it real

Creative & execution

We develop the agreed work in manageable stages, keep you informed, and use your feedback to refine the result.

  • Share drafts and progress at agreed checkpoints
  • Review work together and incorporate feedback
  • Prepare final assets and handover details
04
Learn & adapt

Review & next steps

After delivery or launch, we can review what is working and identify sensible improvements based on available information.

  • Review agreed measures and feedback
  • Capture learnings and open questions
  • Plan next steps only when they make sense
✓

Clear communication at every stage

You should always know the current status, what we need from you, and what the next decision is.
